What School App Does

A complete digital foundation for running your school efficiently.

School Information System

Students, staff, guardians, classes, streams, and academic records — all organized and always up to date.

Resource Management

Fee invoicing, income and expense tracking, and inventory — automated, accurate, and transparent.

School Library

Track books, members, and loans with a simple, reliable system built for busy librarians.

Our Aim

School App exists to reduce the paperwork and guesswork in running a school, so that administrators, teachers, and staff can focus on what matters most — the students. Every record, from a single fee payment to a full academic year, is accurate, searchable, and available the moment you need it.

  • Reduce manual, repetitive record-keeping
  • Give every role exactly the tools they need
  • Keep school data secure and organized

Terms of Use

Please review before using the system.

Access to this system is limited to authorized staff, students, and guardians of the school. Accounts are personal and must not be shared. Each user is responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential.

Users are responsible for the accuracy of the information they enter, including student records, financial transactions, and inventory data. The school reserves the right to review and correct records as needed.

Student, staff, and financial information stored in this system is confidential. It may only be accessed and used for legitimate school purposes, and must not be shared outside the school without proper authorization.

While every effort is made to keep the system available and data safe, the school does not guarantee uninterrupted access and recommends regular review of critical records.
